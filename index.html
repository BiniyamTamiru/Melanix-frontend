Simple Routine

How to Use

1
Cleanse
Begin with clean, slightly damp hair. Wash with a gentle sulfate-free shampoo and towel dry.
2
Apply
Dispense a small amount of Melanix lotion onto your fingertips. Part your hair and apply directly to the scalp.
3
Massage
Gently massage in circular motions for 3–5 minutes to boost circulation and ensure deep absorption.
4
Repeat Daily
Use consistently every morning or evening. Results typically visible within 4–8 weeks of regular use.
